How it works

Using the solver

Type the word you guessed in Wordle, then tap each tile to match the colours Wordle gave you (grey → yellow → green). Tap Add this guess. The page filters its dictionary to the words that still fit and recommends your next guess. To fix a mistake, tap Edit on that round. Later rounds are discarded and everything is recalculated. Your game is saved in this browser, so closing the tab is fine. There’s nothing to quit.

The recommendations

Best answer guess
The remaining word most likely to be the answer. It scores each word on how common its letters are in each position among the words still possible, and favours words from the main answer list.
Best test word
The word that splits the remaining words into the most even groups, so whatever colours come back leave the fewest words. It may not be a possible answer. With 10 or fewer words left it picks the word with the best worst case.
Elimination word
Built from the letters you haven’t tried yet. It targets the five untested letters that appear in about half the remaining words, because those split the list best. It doesn’t look at colours or positions, so it’s a quick way to test many letters at once.
The difference
The test word is the best split using everything known. The elimination word is simply the best use of untested letters.
Best word pairs
Two guesses that, played together, are expected to leave the fewest words.
Letters
Green letters are in the right spot. Wrong-spot letters are in the word. Ruled out letters are grey with no other copy found, and repeats are handled as Wordle does.

Other features

Hard mode only recommends words that reuse every hint. ✕ bans a word Wordle rejected. Words adds a missing word or restores a banned one. Play gives you a random secret word. Each guess shows its colours, plus a g, y or x in the corner and a letter tracker beneath. There’s a Hint button, a Give up button and a Copy result button.

Good to know

Words I don’t know show a warning but still work. Bans and added words are saved per browser. The starting-word list is precomputed for the built-in dictionary.
